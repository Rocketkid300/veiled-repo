🔒 Veiled

✓ Version 2.0.0

Hide any app from your Home Screen on Dopamine (rootless, iOS 15–16). Long-press an app and pick "Add to Veiled". Your hidden apps live inside an app that looks and works like a Calculator — type your passcode and press = to get in.

📲 Install in Sileo

  1. Open Sileo → Sources → ⨁
  2. Add this repo URL: https://rocketkid300.github.io/veiled-repo/
  3. Install Veiled — it also installs Veiled App (the Calculator) and resprings

⚙️ First-time setup

  1. Open Calculator → set a passcode when asked
  2. Hide: long-press any Home Screen app → "Add to Veiled", or tap + inside Veiled to hide several at once
  3. Open Veiled: type your passcode in the calculator and press =
  4. Tap a hidden app to open it, swipe left to unhide

✨ What's inside

  • A real, working calculator as the disguise
  • Hidden apps list with search and a Recent section
  • Private notes with pinning and an optional extra lock (Touch ID or passcode)
  • Encrypted backup & restore of your hidden list and notes
  • Auto-Lock, and a duress code that opens an empty list
  • Close on Lock: Calculator and hidden apps close 5 seconds after the phone locks
  • Low-battery reminder to re-jailbreak, so a dead battery doesn't reveal your apps

If the phone reboots without the jailbreak, no tweak can run and hidden apps reappear until you jailbreak again in Dopamine. Tested on iPhone 7 Plus, iOS 15.8.3.
